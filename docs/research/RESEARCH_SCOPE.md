# Research Scope

**Project:** Pythagoras Circles — Canonical Geometry Research Laboratory  
**Milestone:** M2 Baseline

## 1. Research Objectives

The **Pythagoras Circles — Canonical Geometry Research Laboratory** investigates the structural classification of integer right-angled triangles ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$) organized by the inradius $r$. 

Key research questions:
1. How does the number of primitive triangles $T(r) = 2^{\omega^*(r)}$ and total triangles $N(r) = \frac{\tau(2r^2)}{2}$ depend on the prime factorization of inradius $r$?
2. What is the precise geometric and arithmetic mapping between divisor pairs $xy = 2r^2$ and Euclidean generation parameters $(k, m, n)$ / $(u, v)$?
3. How can continuous geometric constructions (four exterior squares, incircle, Thales circumcircle, incenter partition) be unified with discrete number-theoretic classification?
