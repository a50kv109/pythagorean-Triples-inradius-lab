# Verification Policy

**Project:** Pythagoras Triples Inradius Classification Laboratory  
**Milestone:** M4 Frozen Baseline + `M5-S1` & `M5-S2` (`IMPLEMENTED — PENDING INDEPENDENT AUDIT`)

## 1. Core Verification Principles

- **Exact Arithmetic:** All discrete core routines (`src/core/exact/`, `src/core/factorization/`, `src/core/classification/`, `src/core/facets/`, `src/core/passports/`) operate strictly on native `BigInt` and immutable exact rationals (`ExactRational`). Floating-point approximations (`Math.*`, `Number()`, `parseFloat`, `parseInt`) and `BigInt`-to-`number` conversions are forbidden in computational logic.
- **Separation of Concerns:** Exact number-theoretic, classification, facet, passport, and matrix calculations are cleanly separated from legacy continuous geometry models (`src/math/`) and visual screen rendering (`M7`).
- **Primality & Completeness Safety:** Probable primality (`PROBABLE_PRIME`) is never conflated with proven primality (`PROVEN_PRIME`). Incomplete factorizations or classifications (`isComplete === false`) are reported explicitly and rejected across M4 and `M5-S2` `ClassMatrix` (`INCOMPLETE_CLASSIFICATION_RESULT`). In `M5-S1` (`ClassPassport`), computational completeness (`status: 'COMPLETE' | 'INCOMPLETE'`, `isComplete`) is explicitly separated from M2 mathematical certification (`certificationStatus`), while partial M2 data is preserved on `INCOMPLETE` with `profile = null`, `subclassSpectrum = []`, and `expectedMatrixSummary = { rowCount: null, isSortedByX: false, firstTripleId: null, lastTripleId: null }`.
- **Population Isolation, Passport & Matrix Consistency (M4-S3 / M5-S1 / M5-S2):** Class aggregates strictly separate all triples ($N(r)$) from primitive triples ($T(r)$); Berggren depth bounds are computed strictly on primitive triples ($k = 1$). `M5-S1` enforces `CP-INV-01..08` (including `CP-INV-07` rejection of corrupted `isSortedByX` on `INCOMPLETE` passports), strict ascending `x` ordering of `classification.allTriples` for `COMPLETE` passports, and exact `{x0, y0} = {v², 2u²}` primitive-shift validation in `EuclidAdapter`. `M5-S2` enforces `CM-INV-01..08`—where `CM-INV-07` (BERGGREN mathematical identity) verifies not only internal `canonWord`/`rawWord` consistency but also exact reconstruction of the row's `CORE` primitive ancestor via M4-S2 `applyCanonWord()` and `applyRawWord()`, and facet caches are accepted only upon full identity correspondence with the matching M3 triple and M4 invariant verification (`FACET_CACHE_MISMATCH`)—alongside deterministic shallow-copy `x` normalization, `Q1 = CLOSED` (`asymQ` inherited from `M4-S1`), `Full === Lazy` parity, and joint `verifyPassportMatrixAlignment`.
- **Golden Benchmarks & Regression Tests:** The automated suite (`npm test`, 10 test scripts including `classPassportM5S1.test.ts` and `classMatrixM5S2.test.ts`) verifies golden totals for $r = 1\dots 30$ ($T=61, N=174$), $r = 1\dots 50$ ($110$ unique Berggren primitive paths, $339$ total rows), $r = 1\dots 100$ ($T=249, N=855$), and $r = 1\dots 120$ ($120$ classes, $N=1098, T=311$), alongside Golden matrices up to $r = 420$ ($81$ rows). *(Note: M5-S1 and M5-S2 remain `IMPLEMENTED — PENDING INDEPENDENT AUDIT`.)* See [`M4_VERIFICATION_AND_TESTS.md`](M4_VERIFICATION_AND_TESTS.md).
