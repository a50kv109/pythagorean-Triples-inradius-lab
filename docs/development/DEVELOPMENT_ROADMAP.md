# Development Roadmap

**Project:** Pythagoras Circles — Canonical Geometry Research Laboratory  
**Milestone:** M2 Baseline

## Milestone Plan

- **Phase 1, 1.1, 1.2:** Completed — Lab shell, UI refinement, navigation, and initial architectural audit.
- **M1 (Exact Computation Core):** Completed — Autonomous BigInt arithmetic, rational numbers, radius primitives.
- **M2 (Number Theory Engine):** Completed & Stabilized — Prime factorization, primality certification, divisor generation, hyperbola pairs, arithmetic counters.
- **M3 (Classification Engine):** Planned — Triangle class $C(r)$ and sub-class $C_k(r)$ builder and generator.
- **M4 (Facets & Structural Analysis):** Planned — Sporn coordinates, Berggren trees, multi-attribute matrices.
- **M5 (Passports & Matrices):** Planned — Class passports and multi-attribute structural catalogs.
- **M6 (Independent Verifier):** Planned — Independent automated verifier checking `CHK-01..12`.
- **M7 (Geometric Projection):** Planned — SVG projection and visual adapter translating exact integers to screen coordinates.
- **M8 (Export & Analytics):** Planned — JSON/CSV export, advanced reporting, and workspace analytics.
